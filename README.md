# Word Counter

## A word counter written in Go.

Takes a sentence of text and counts the total number of words, along with the occurrence of each word.

| Part | Status |
|------|--------|
|Hardcore a paragraph of text.| Done|
|Lowercase it, split it into words, and strip punctuation.| Done|
|Count each word's frequency.| Done|
|Count the total amount of words.| Done|
|Print the total amound of words and every word with it's count.| Done|
